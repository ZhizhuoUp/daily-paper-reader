## 摘要
本文研究了具有时变代价函数的线性动力系统的预测性在线控制问题。该研究受机动激光雷达（LiDAR）传感系统实时自适应控制等应用的启发，在这些应用中，控制器必须在线调整传感方向，以在不断变化的环境条件下平衡定位精度、扫描效率和平滑驱动。我们提出了一种预测性在线控制（POC）算法，该算法利用未来代价函数的短期预测，同时考虑了由系统动力学引起的记忆效应。通过使用扰动-动作控制器（DAC）参数化，在线控制问题被转化为策略参数上的带记忆在线凸优化（OCO-with-memory）公式。我们开发了一种窗口化后退视界更新方法，该方法结合了短期预测，并引入了总变分正则化项以抑制策略的剧烈波动。在理论上，我们证明了 POC 能够实现与比较序列路径长度成比例的动态策略遗憾界，并提供了关于对数预测视界和记忆视界的遗憾保证充分条件。所提方法在机动激光雷达传感任务上进行了评估，结果表明该方法在提高定位精度的同时，在传感精度和扫描完整性之间实现了良好的权衡。

## Abstract
This paper studies predictive online control for linear dynamical systems with time-varying cost functions, motivated by applications such as real-time adaptive control of motorized LiDAR sensing systems, where the controller must adjust the sensing direction online to balance localization accuracy, scanning efficiency, and smooth actuation under changing environmental conditions. We propose a predictive online control (POC) algorithm that leverages short-term predictions of future cost functions while accounting for the memory effect induced by system dynamics. Using the disturbance-action controller (DAC) parameterization, the online control problem is transformed into an OCO-with-memory formulation over policy parameters. We develop a windowed receding-horizon update that incorporates short-term predictions and accommodates a total-variation regularizer to suppress abrupt policy variations. Theoretically, we prove that POC achieves a dynamic policy regret bound scaling with the path length of the comparator sequence, and provide sufficient conditions in terms of logarithmic prediction and memory horizons for the regret guarantee. The proposed method is evaluated on a motorized LiDAR sensing task, demonstrating improved localization accuracy together with a favorable trade-off between sensing accuracy and scanning completeness.
