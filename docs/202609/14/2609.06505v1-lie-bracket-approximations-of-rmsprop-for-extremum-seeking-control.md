## 摘要
本文提出了一种受 RMSProp 动力学启发并基于李括号近似技术的极值搜索新算法。为了继承经典连续时间 RMSProp 算法的行为，我们引入了一种利用二阶李括号的极值搜索方案，从而允许在所得的李括号系统中激发平方梯度项。此外，我们提出了两种仅需使用一阶李括号近似即可实现的改进型 RMSProp 类算法，从而降低了极值搜索控制设计的复杂度。最后，通过数值仿真对这三种极值搜索算法进行了比较。

## Abstract
This paper presents novel extremum seeking algorithms inspired by RMSProp dynamics and based on Lie bracket approximation techniques. To inherit the behavior of the classical continuous-time RMSProp algorithm, we introduce an extremum seeking scheme that exploits second-order Lie brackets, allowing the excitation of squared gradient terms in the resulting Lie bracket system. Moreover, we propose two modified RMSProp-type algorithms that can be implemented using only first-order Lie bracket approximations, thereby reducing the complexity of the extremum seeking control design. The three resulting extremum seeking algorithms are compared through numerical simulations.
