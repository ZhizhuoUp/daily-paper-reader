## 摘要
本文提出了一种针对多旋翼无人机在局部空间扰动下运行的电池感知预测轨迹规划与控制框架。通过闭环车辆-电机-电池传播对候选轨迹进行评估，将扰动引起的控制需求、电能消耗、电池状态演变以及依赖于端电压的执行器能力纳入规划过程。通过与独立实现的 Simscape 等效电路参考模型进行数值基准测试，该降阶电池模型的功率归一化均方根误差（NRMSE）为 0.64%，累积能量偏差低于 0.7%。在包含三个扰动区域、时长 150 秒、航程 640 米的任务中，所选轨迹相较于扰动感知固定参考基准，电能消耗降低了 7.46%，位置跟踪均方根误差（RMSE）降低了约 72%。规划器消融实验表明，电池相关项在荷电状态（SOC）正常时影响较小，但在电池电量耗尽的压力条件下会改变所选轨迹。通过多种反馈控制器的执行测试进一步证明，控制器的选择会改变跟踪精度、能耗和执行器利用率之间的权衡关系。研究结果证明了在轨迹选择过程中考虑预测的闭环能量及电池-执行器影响的优势。

## Abstract
This paper presents a battery-aware predictive trajectory-planning and control framework for multirotors operating under spatially localized disturbances. Candidate trajectories are evaluated through closed-loop vehicle--motor--battery propagation, allowing disturbance-induced control demand, electrical energy, battery evolution, and terminal-voltage-dependent actuator capability to enter the planning process. % A reduced-order battery model is numerically benchmarked against an independently implemented Simscape equivalent-circuit reference, with a power NRMSE of $0.64\%$ and a cumulative-energy discrepancy below $0.7\%$. % In a $150$-s, $640$-m mission containing three disturbance regions, the selected trajectory reduces electrical energy consumption by $7.46\%$ and position-tracking RMSE by approximately $72\%$ relative to the disturbance-aware fixed-reference baseline. % Planner ablations show that battery-dependent terms are nonbinding at nominal SOC but alter the selected trajectory under a depleted-battery stress condition. % Execution with multiple feedback controllers further demonstrates that controller selection changes the tradeoff among tracking accuracy, energy consumption, and actuator utilization. % The results demonstrate the benefit of accounting for predicted closed-loop energetic and battery--actuator consequences during trajectory selection.
