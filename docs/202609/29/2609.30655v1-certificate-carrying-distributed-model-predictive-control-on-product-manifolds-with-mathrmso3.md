## 摘要
本文研究了当相邻预测在采样时刻之间发生变化时，同步分布式模型预测控制（DMPC）中的约束认证问题。在并行局部求解之前，每个智能体都会通信一个移位预测和一个声明的更新预算。硬轨迹信任域使得该预算具有可执行性，而从移位数据包计算出的边际可行性上限则在不使用任何当前优化器输出的情况下保持了回退可行性。距离和相对姿态约束通过显式的 Lipschitz 常数和两个预算层进行了收紧：一层用于处理同步的邻居更新，另一层保留了可检查的移位储备。我们证明了在既定的标称执行和终端假设下，硬成对约束满足和递归可行性，给出了由执行误差引起的额外残差，并推导了局部实际值下降界限。航天器编队算例使用了硬终端和成对约束、$\mathrm{SO}(3)$ 上的测地线相对姿态约束以及可重现的终端集检查。与固定边际、仅轨迹边际和窗口化在线边际的比较表明，所提出的预算在保持正向移位可行性边际的同时，降低了保守性。

## Abstract
This paper studies constraint certification in synchronous distributed model predictive control (DMPC) when neighboring predictions change between sampling instants. Before the parallel local solves, each agent communicates a shifted prediction and an announced update budget. A hard trajectory trust region makes that budget enforceable, while an edge-wise feasibility cap computed from the shifted packets keeps the fallback feasible without using any current optimizer output. Distance and relative-attitude constraints are tightened with explicit Lipschitz constants and two budget layers: one accounts for the simultaneous neighbor update and the other retains a checkable shift reserve. We prove hard pairwise constraint satisfaction and recursive feasibility under stated nominal-execution and terminal assumptions, give the additional residual caused by execution error, and derive a local practical value-decrease bound. A spacecraft formation example uses hard terminal and pairwise constraints, a geodesic relative- attitude constraint on $\SO$, and reproducible terminal-set checks. Comparisons with fixed, trajectory-only, and windowed online margins show that the proposed budget reduces conservatism while preserving a positive shifted-feasibility margin.
