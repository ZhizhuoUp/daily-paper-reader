## 摘要
我们提出了FAVOR（自由漂浮机械臂递归灵敏度速度优化），这是一种用于无驱动航天器上无碰撞到达、跟踪及规定时间预抓取拦截的规划器。该方法通过线性速度、加速度和连续性约束来优化贝塞尔关节速度曲线。决策维度与展开分辨率无关。解析递归灵敏度提供了通过耦合基座-机械臂运动的任务梯度和间隙梯度。并行评估、缓存和增量碰撞检测减少了计算量。在七自由度机械臂和五个模拟航天器模型的测试中，FAVOR实现了99.8%的点对点成功率，平均计算时间为1.779秒，而基于逆运动学（IK）初始化的位置样条基准方法的成功率仅为62.9%，平均计算时间为50.270秒（平均值包含失败和超时情况）。FAVOR完成了36个跟踪案例中的30个，而单步二次规划（QP）仅完成15个；在拦截任务中，FAVOR完成了全部36个实例，而样条基准方法仅完成22个。受控消融实验表明，有限差分法使平均规划时间增加了4.7倍。

## Abstract
We present FAVOR (Free-floating Arm Velocity Optimization with Recursive Sensitivities), a planner for collision-free reaching, tracking, and prescribed-time pre-grasp interception on an unactuated spacecraft. It optimizes Bezier joint-velocity curves with linear velocity, acceleration, and continuity constraints. Decision dimension is independent of rollout resolution. Analytical recursive sensitivities provide task and clearance gradients through the coupled base-arm motion. Parallel evaluation, caching, and incremental collision discovery reduce computation. With a seven-DoF arm and five simulated spacecraft models, FAVOR achieves 99.8% point-to-point success with 1.779 s mean computation, versus 62.9% and 50.270 s for an IK-initialized position-spline baseline. Means include failures and timeouts. FAVOR completes 30 of 36 tracking cases, versus 15 for single-step QP, and all 36 interception instances, versus 22 for the spline baseline. A controlled ablation shows that finite differences increase mean planning time 4.7-fold.
