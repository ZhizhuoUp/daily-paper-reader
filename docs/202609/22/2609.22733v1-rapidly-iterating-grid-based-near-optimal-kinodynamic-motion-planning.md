## 摘要
本文提出了快速迭代运动动力学网格（Rapidly-iterating kinoDynamic Grid, RDG）算法，这是一种渐近近最优的动力学约束运动规划算法，能够通过快速迭代生成高质量解。该算法利用状态空间网格分解，在相对于轨迹树节点数量呈常数时间复杂度的前提下执行节点选择、动力学传播和图修正。通过覆盖球序列归纳证明，该算法被证明具有渐近近最优性和概率完备性。论文评估了该算法的不同子系统在产生探索偏置方面相对于基于最近邻搜索的常用方法的表现。在最高达 10 个自由度（DOF）的复杂动力学约束运动规划问题环境中，将 RDG 算法与具有最优性保证的同类稀疏动力学规划算法 SST 和 DIRT 进行了仿真试验对比。RDG 算法在平均最终解质量上分别超越 SST 和 DIRT 高达 104% 和 40%。此外，即便在 SST 和 DIRT 均未能成功的 10 自由度测试用例中，RDG 依然保持了 100% 的成功率。

## Abstract
This paper develops the Rapidly-iterating kinoDynamic Grid (RDG) algorithm, an asymptotically near-optimal kinodynamic motion planning algorithm that produces high quality solutions through rapid iteration. The algorithm leverages a state space grid decomposition to perform node selection, dynamics propagation, and graph revision in constant time complexity with respect to the number of nodes in the trajectory tree. Through a covering ball sequence induction proof, the algorithm is shown to be asymptotically near-optimal and probabilistically complete. Different subsystems of the algorithm are evaluated against common nearest-neighbor search-based methods at generating exploration bias. The RDG algorithm is evaluated through simulated trials in complex, kinodynamic motion planning problem environments up to 10 DOF relative to similar sparse, kinodynamic planning algorithms with optimality guarantees, SST and DIRT. The RDG algorithm outperforms both SST and DIRT in mean final solution quality by up to 104% and 40% respectively. Additionally, RDG maintained a 100% success rate, even on a 10-DOF test case where both SST and DIRT did not.
