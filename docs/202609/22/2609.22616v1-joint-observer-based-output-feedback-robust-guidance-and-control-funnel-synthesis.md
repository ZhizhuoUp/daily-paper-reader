## 摘要
针对受有界过程扰动和测量噪声影响的离散时间非线性系统，本文开发了一种联合制导与基于观测器的鲁棒输出反馈综合框架。该方法协同设计了参考轨迹、时变龙伯格（Luenberger）观测器、基于观测器的线性输出反馈增益，以及用于保证跟踪与估计误差条件不变界的耦合椭球漏斗。非线性残差通过由局部 Lipschitz 常数参数化的增量二次约束（QC）进行界定，该常数借助采样方法估计得到。耦合稳定性条件被构建为线性矩阵不等式（LMI）代理，并通过半定规划（SDP）采用带近端正则化的序列凸规划（prox-SCP）求解。在持续传感器噪声和系统扰动下对单轮车模型的仿真表明，系统相对于联合设计的参考轨迹实现了鲁棒跟踪，并满足了漏斗认证的约束。

## Abstract
A joint guidance and robust observer-based output-feedback synthesis framework is developed for discrete-time nonlinear systems subject to bounded process disturbances and measurement noise. The method co-designs a reference trajectory, time-varying Luenberger observer and observer-based linear output-feedback gains, and coupled ellipsoidal funnels that certify conditioned-invariant bounds on tracking and estimation errors. Nonlinear residuals are bounded by incremental quadratic constraints (QCs) parameterized by local Lipschitz constants, estimated via sampling approaches. The coupled stability conditions are formulated as linear matrix inequality (LMI) surrogates and solved using sequential convex programming with proximal regularization (prox-SCP) via semidefinite programming (SDP). Simulations of a unicycle model under persistent sensory noise and system disturbances demonstrate robust tracking and funnel-certified constraint satisfaction with respect to the jointly designed reference trajectory.
