## 摘要
本文研究了自动驾驶车辆（AV）在存在移动障碍物且通过毫米波（mmWave）信道进行通信的动态环境下的节能运行问题。障碍物会导致毫米波信道严重衰减，从而形成高度动态的通信环境。在此背景下，我们考虑了自动驾驶车辆在动态障碍物中安全导航至指定目的地，同时确保车载传感或遥测数据通过毫米波信道及时传输的运动与通信能量联合优化问题。我们寻求一种实时方法，以在动态障碍物既引起安全约束又导致时变毫米波阻塞、从而产生紧密耦合的运动-通信权衡的环境中，计算节能轨迹。我们提出了一种非线性模型预测控制（NMPC）框架，该框架实现了预见性的通信与运动决策及能量协同优化，并辅以控制障碍函数（CBF）以确保安全性。大量的仿真结果证明了该方法的有效性，与基准策略相比，总能耗降低了高达 37.3%。总体而言，我们的结果表明，所提出的基于 NMPC 的框架显著提高了自动驾驶车辆在动态、对阻塞敏感的毫米波通信约束下的能源效率和性能。

## Abstract
This paper studies energy-efficient operation of autonomous vehicles (AVs) in dynamic environments with moving obstacles and while communicating over mmWave channels. The obstacles induce severe attenuation of the mmWave channel resulting in a highly dynamic communication environment. In this setting, we consider the problem of jointly optimizing motion and communication energy for an AV that safely navigates among dynamic obstacles toward a designated destination while ensuring timely transmission of onboard sensing or telemetry data over mmWave channels. We then seek a real-time methodology to compute energy-efficient trajectories in a setting where dynamic obstacles induce both safety constraints and time-varying mmWave blockage, leading to tightly coupled motion-communication trade-offs. We propose a nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC) framework that enables anticipative communication and motion decision-making and energy co-optimization, augmented with a control barrier function (CBF) to ensure safety. Extensive simulation results demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach, reducing total energy consumption by up to 37.3% compared to baseline strategies. Overall, our results demonstrate that the proposed NMPC-based framework significantly enhances energy efficiency and performance of AVs under dynamic, blockage-sensitive mmWave communication constraints.
