## 摘要
采用移动接收机的多基站测距由于结合了自适应传感器几何结构，能够实现良好的跟踪性能。然而，在包含信号遮挡障碍物的环境中，需要进行接收机轨迹规划，以保持与发射机及目标对象之间的视距（LOS）条件。在本论文中，我们为多基站跟踪开发了一种非短视的收缩时界框架。该框架利用凸避障约束和以维持良好 LOS 信号条件为重点的控制目标，并考虑了未来的遮挡情况。我们通过数值实验证明了该方法的效率和跟踪精度。

## Abstract
Multistatic ranging with mobile receivers enables good tracking performance due to the combined adaptive sensor geometry. However, environments that contain signal obstructing obstacles require receiver trajectory planning to maintain line-of-sight (LOS) conditions with transmitters and the target of interest. In this letter, we develop a non-myopic receding-horizon framework for multistatic tracking. It uses convex collision-avoidance constraints and a control objective that focuses on maintaining good LOS signal conditions, taking into account future obstructions. We demonstrate the efficiency and tracking accuracy of the method via a numerical experiment.
